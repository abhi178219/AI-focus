## Alternatives considered:
1. Use Experian_PersonalLoans.docx as baseline (rejected — over-fit to an expired, over-cap role).
2. Maintain multiple resume variants (payments-led, wealth-led, lending-led) and score against the closest variant per role (deferred — see H3/H4 hypotheses; not yet justified without user-approved edits).
3. Use FINAL_Experian.docx as the single general-purpose baseline for scoring, and propose role-specific tweaks on top of it (chosen).

## Reasoning:
FINAL_Experian.docx is the most comprehensive/general version and gives the most accurate baseline fit score across varied domains. Role-specific tweaks are then proposed per role (per SKILL.md output spec) rather than assuming a pre-tailored variant. This keeps scoring consistent scan-to-scan and avoids drift from maintaining multiple unapproved variants.

## Trade-offs accepted:
- Fit scores may be slightly conservative for roles where a tailored variant (once user-approved) would score higher (e.g., a wealth-led variant per H4 could push Tide Wealth Services above 86%).
- If the user approves tweaks for a recurring role type (e.g., Tide Wealth Services or Applied Data Finance-style AI lending), consider creating a dedicated resume variant and updating this decision.
